# Stories Product Home

> Stories helps people turn book learnings and lived experiences into memories they can retrieve and reuse—privately, from ordinary local Markdown.

## The Product in One Minute

- **The Problem**: People save ideas from books and lessons from life, but retrieval fails when those ideas would actually be useful. Notes apps wait for you to remember to search; flashcard apps feel like homework.
- **The Promise**: *Keep what mattered. Remember it when it can matter again.*
- **The Core Loop**: **Capture → Cue → Attempt → Reveal → Rate → Reuse**
- **The Experience**: A calm, Android-first native app centered around Today, Library, and Settings.
- **Data Ownership**: App-private UTF-8 Markdown files on your device. Zero accounts, zero tracking, 100% offline.
- **Explicit Non-Goals**: No AI chat, no global graph dashboards, no cloud sync lock-in, no social feeds, no task lists or habit streaks.

## Key Product Pillars (PM & UI/UX)

1. **Memory Before Notes**: The daily surface is for remembering and capturing. The Library is durable infrastructure, not a busy filing dashboard.
2. **First-Session Value (Aha Moment)**: New users should experience the full recall loop on day one—via an immediate practice prompt or a 1-day initial recall—rather than waiting 3 days in silence.
3. **Attempt Before Answer**: When a memory returns, the cue prompts active retrieval while the stored text remains hidden.
4. **Warmth Over Machinery**: Personalized cue prompts ("What idea from *Atomic Habits* stayed with you?") replace sterile system prompts.
5. **Calm Over Compulsion**: No streaks, no overdue warnings, no scores, no guilt.
6. **Honest Portability**: Human-readable Markdown under the hood with 1-click export so users truly own their data.
