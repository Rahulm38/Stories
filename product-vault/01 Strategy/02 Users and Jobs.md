# Users and Jobs

## Primary job

**When I notice something worth keeping, help me remember it well enough that it comes to mind naturally when I want to tell or use it later.**

## Important material

The user does not need to classify any of this. The same simple capture supports:

- something that happened to me;
- a funny or surprising moment;
- something another person said or did;
- a useful observation;
- an idea or lesson;
- a fact or quote worth having available in conversation.

## Functional jobs

- Capture before a moment disappears.
- Find a memory later by rough fragments such as a person, place, topic, or phrase.
- Get a useful clue without seeing the answer.
- Rehearse telling the memory naturally.
- See the original when recall fails.
- Tell Stories whether the memory was available.
- Stop resurfacing something without deleting it.

## Emotional jobs

- Feel more prepared and interesting in everyday conversation without performing or studying.
- Trust that saved memories remain private and unchanged.
- Avoid guilt, backlogs, streak pressure, and complicated organisation.

## Design implications

The user should never need to understand categories, scheduling algorithms, storage structures, cue authoring, or learning terminology. The interface teaches the product through the loop itself.
