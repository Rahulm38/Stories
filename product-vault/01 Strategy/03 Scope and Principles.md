# Scope and Principles

## Product principles

1. Capture must feel easier than deciding not to capture.
2. The original memory is always the source of truth.
3. Stories helps users remember and tell; it never silently invents, embellishes, or rewrites their history.
4. Organisation is the system's job, not the user's.
5. A clue should trigger a memory without giving away the answer.
6. Speaking and natural recall matter more than exact wording.
7. Resurfacing should feel useful, never like homework.
8. Good storytelling develops through small repetitions rather than tutorials.
9. Every additional field must justify its friction.
10. Privacy is part of the core product.

## Current scope

- One-step text capture.
- Automatic first return after a few days.
- Deterministic story cues built only from the user's original text.
- Tell-before-reveal flow.
- Three simple outcomes: Not yet / Mostly / Yes.
- Progressive future returns.
- Five-memory session cap.
- Tomorrow and Stop resurfacing controls.
- Plain edit/share/delete.
- Search across title and memory content with multi-word matching.
- Quiet optional reminders.
- Local app-private storage and no account.

## Explicit non-goals for now

- User-visible memory types or categories.
- Manual cue fields.
- Formatting toolbars or syntax-based editing.
- Reflection forms.
- Streaks, scores, leaderboards, or overdue debt.
- Automatic story rewriting.
- Large storytelling courses or rigid narrative templates.
- Social feed or public publishing.
