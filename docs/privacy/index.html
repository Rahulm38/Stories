Privacy Policy

Stories

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

Stories is a local-first note and recall mobile application. It stores the notes you create as Markdown files in the app's private storage on your device.

Data Handling

  • No Account Required: Stories does not require an account or sign-in.
  • No Data Collection: Stories does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your notes or personal data off your device.
  • No Tracking: Stories does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Disabled Cloud Backup: Android cloud backup is explicitly disabled for Stories to keep your data local to your device.

The app accesses local notes strictly on-device to provide note capture, library management, wikilink resolution, note editing, and offline recall features.

Retention & Deletion

Your notes remain on your device until you edit or delete them, clear the application storage, or uninstall the app. Because Stories does not transmit or receive your notes, the developer cannot retrieve, view, or delete your data remotely.

Children's Privacy

Stories is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions).

Policy Changes

If a future release introduces accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or updated data practices, this privacy policy and the corresponding Google Play Data Safety declarations will be updated before such a release is distributed.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please contact the developer via the official support contact listed on the Stories Google Play Store listing.
